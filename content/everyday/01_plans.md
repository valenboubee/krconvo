## A. Context

Two people who are dating are texting after work. One of them wants to relax at
home tonight and figure out what to eat. Listen for how they **suggest** ideas,
**disagree gently**, and **decide together** — all in casual speech (반말).

## B. Conversation

**수민:** 오빠, 오늘 저녁에 뭐 해?
> Hey, what are you doing tonight?

**지훈:** 별거 없어. 왜, 뭐 하고 싶어?
> Nothing much. Why, what do you want to do?

**수민:** 그냥 집에서 쉬고 싶은데, 뭐 좀 시켜 먹을까?
> I just kind of want to relax at home — should we order something to eat?

**지훈:** 좋아. 근데 어제도 치킨 먹었잖아. 오늘은 다른 거 먹자.
> Sounds good. But we had chicken yesterday too, remember? Let's eat something different today.

**수민:** 그럼 떡볶이 어때? 요즘 매운 게 계속 당겨.
> Then how about tteokbokki? I've been craving spicy food lately.

**지훈:** 오, 좋다. 나도 매운 거 완전 좋아하잖아. 그럼 떡볶이 시키자.
> Oh, nice. You know I love spicy food too. Let's order tteokbokki then.

**수민:** 알겠어. 내가 시킬게. 오빠는 영화 하나 골라 놔.
> Okay. I'll order it — you go pick a movie.

**지훈:** 콜. 그럼 밥 오는 동안 뭐 볼지 정하자.
> Deal. Then let's decide what to watch while the food's coming.

## D. Language You Need

### -ㄹ까? — "Should we...? / Shall I...?"

**Meaning:** Softly suggest an idea or ask what the other person thinks.

**Used here:**

> 뭐 좀 시켜 먹을까?

**Basic shape:**

> verb stem + -(으)ㄹ까?

**What changes:**
- 먹다 → 먹을까? (should we eat?)
- 보다 → 볼까? (should we watch?)

[[respond]]
Q: Suggest it: "Should we watch a movie?"
A: 영화 볼까?
[[/respond]]

### -잖아 — "You know / remember, ..."

**Meaning:** Remind the listener of something you both already know. It sounds
natural and a little insistent, common between close people.

**Used here:**

> 어제도 치킨 먹었잖아.

**What changes:**
- Attach -잖아 to the plain form: 먹었다 → 먹었잖아, 좋아하다 → 좋아하잖아.

[[respond]]
Q: Remind them: "You know I don't like spicy food." (Use -잖아, casual)
A: 나 매운 거 안 좋아하잖아.
[[/respond]]

### -자 — "Let's ..."

**Meaning:** Casual way to suggest doing something together.

**Used here:**

> 오늘은 다른 거 먹자.

[[respond]]
Q: Say it casually: "Let's decide later."
A: 나중에 정하자.
[[/respond]]

## G. Transfer

Same language, new situation: it's the weekend and you're deciding what to do
together — not what to eat.

[[respond]]
Q: Suggest, casually: "Should we go out somewhere today?"
A: 오늘 어디 나갈까?
[[/respond]]

[[respond]]
Q: Gently disagree using -잖아: "But we went out yesterday too."
A: 근데 어제도 나갔잖아.
[[/respond]]
